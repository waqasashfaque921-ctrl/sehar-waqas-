Dil Ki Baat

Meri Shehzadi Sehar,

Jab se tum meri zindagi mein aayi ho, mere ghar ki deewarein bhi muskurana seekh gayi hain. Tumhari ek awaaz se thakan utar jaati hai, aur tumhari khamoshi mein bhi mujhe sukoon milta hai.

Main aksar sochta hoon ke Allah ne meri jholi mein tumhein kyun rakha. Shayad is liye ke main seekh sakoon ke mohabbat sirf kehne ka naam nahi, nibhane ka naam hai. Tumne mujhe sabr diya, narmi di, aur wo hausla diya jo mere paas kabhi tha hi nahi.

Tum jahan bhi hoti ho, meri izzat aur meri dua tumhare saath chalti hai. Tum sirf meri biwi nahi, meri hamsafar, meri raazdaar, aur meri sab se pyaari dost ho.

Meri Jaan, tumhari chhoti chhoti baatein, chai ke saath tumhari hansi, meri fikr mein tumhara jaagna, ye sab mere liye kisi khazaane se kam nahi. Tum meri zindagi ho, isi liye main tumhein Ant Al-Hayat kehta hoon.
